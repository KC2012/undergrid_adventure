# Alpha 9 quick reference

Four travel together: Rook and three companions. Keys **1–4**, portraits, or Tab select them. Visit a district with a safe bunk to use the roster: bench someone, then bring a reserve companion in. Equipment, talents and bonds stay with them.

**Space** jumps in exploration. Hold A/D for momentum; ordinary low cover and small hatch openings are vaulted automatically. **S+Space**, or touch **DROP**, descends through a maintenance shelf or a nearby real hatch. Solid steel cannot be dropped through. W/S climbs; climbing through a hatch dismounts onto its rim. Click a floor for an automatic walk, ladder, jump or drop route. The Ward’s maintenance shelves are a safe introduction.

In combat, select a destination floor. Its preview includes jump arcs and drop labels; moving spends the displayed AP. On touch, confirm the preview with a second tap. Space still fires in combat; touch JUMP explains how to select a tactical jump destination. S+Space or DROP uses the nearest designated opening. Watching companions hold position.

Shots, hostile targeting, grenade lobs and blasts respect actual steel and real holes. A ladder attached to a deck’s side does not automatically create a firing aperture. A winch needs a clear cable line: **T**, then select a cyan anchor. It moves only the selected fighter for **2 AP** in combat. During exploration, crew within range and with a clear cable line winch together; others need an approach. Saving waits until the crew lands.

Look for scratches under the upper Cableworks service panel. Any crew can repair its latch for three salvage; Orren or Latch saves those parts. Inside, a paper survey works without an engineer, while repairing the terminal also annotates the atlas. Nim notices seams farther away. Talk to Brass on the Ashline west deck and Ilex on the Furnace west deck. Their personal work appears at the usual work boards while they travel with you. The Ward board is on the ground concourse at x720.

The Ashline checkpoint can be fought through, negotiated with a tenants’ receipt and Brass or +2 standing, or opened with a ¢45 public bond. Sable can help the Cableworks courier without a medkit; Ilex or Orren can free the Sump hoist without parts. Each encounter pays once.

# Alpha 8 quick reference

**M / MAP** opens the Explorer’s Atlas. The world page connects observed exits; the local page gradually records floors, ladders and landmarks. Unknown passages stay unnamed. Select a discovered destination for a route explanation, add a pin/note, or zoom the current local survey. Planning does not move the crew. [Atlas guide](ATLAS.md).

Before firing, inspect chance, damage on hit, AP, ammunition, range, cover, armor and effects. Hover a floor for the actual movement path and AP cost; cyan is valid, red is not. Cover protects from the side facing the attacker; flanking, high ground and destroying it matter. Floors still block fire, so find a useful edge when shooting down.

Touch combat: tap a hostile to inspect, then tap again to fire; tap a floor to preview its route, then tap the destination again to move. Keyboard Q/Space and the crew dock remain available. NEXT cycles nearby interactions during exploration.

SMG, heavy, burst-rifle and Disorder users can **SUPPRESS** for two AP/two rounds. It applies suppression without direct damage. Overwatch commits the fighter to one movement-triggered reaction. Watching companions stay put. Steam, chemicals, electrical equipment and explosive barrels are tactical features; barrel blasts can hurt allies. The Annex isolator works during combat for one AP. [Combat rules and balance](ALPHA_8_COMBAT.md).

Focus loss does not open a menu. Notes made during combat are saved after the encounter, following the usual save rule.

## Combat

Nearby enemies trigger a local encounter when a crew member approaches within 380 world units. Most districts use nearby patrol groups. Bell Engine, Blacksite, convoy, excavator and dispatched jobs engage the full site. Approaching another hostile or attacking it can add it to a fight. Each living crew member starts with 3 AP per round; you may alternate between them. Click a connected floor to preview and execute a walk/ladder/drop/hop route. Base movement costs 1 AP per 240 route units, rounded up; armor, gear, talents and weapon mobility change this allowance (shown in PACK). Choose shorter intermediate destinations when a route exceeds your available AP.

Shot chances include weapon accuracy, effective range, distance, level, suppression, cover and elevation. Steel platforms block firing lines through them. Crates protect a nearby target when they are on the shooter-facing side. Misses suppress targets; suppressed crew lose one AP on the next round. Shotguns also suppress and push enemies back on their floor. Range differs substantially between weapons; the long rifle costs two AP.

Barrels and frags damage allies as well as enemies, can trigger other barrels, and destroy crates. Be careful where you stand. Overwatch spends all remaining AP and permits one reaction shot during hostile movement if line of sight and ammo allow it. A shot consumes ammunition even if it misses.

Wire hounds rush into melee. Breachers close to shotgun range. Riflemen seek longer lanes. Wounded enemies stay wounded if you retreat. To retreat, move the selected crew member within 180 units of the encounter entry, open M, and select RETREAT. The crew returns to Disintegration Ward.

Defeated story hostiles award XP and credits once; clearing a local encounter grants ammunition and salvage. The district remains active until all patrols are gone. Levels increase maximum HP and grant partial recovery. Fallen crew recover to 30% HP after victory. A full defeat returns you to your last bunk checkpoint with 75% HP and costs 15% of credits; discoveries and world wounds remain. Disintegration Ward's bunk heals freely. If completely out of ammunition, click a hostile within 70 units to strike with a knife for 1 AP.

## Preparing the crew

Ivo trades from the settlement's market balcony. The crew shares spare ammunition, medkits, grenades and credits. Each weapon keeps its own current magazine only while equipped; swapping unloads its rounds into reserves and loads the new gun from reserves. It never grants free ammo. All ranged weapons use the shared ammunition resource intentionally. The chainblade uses no rounds.

The first two allies are Vera, a carbine courier, and Orren, an armored shotgun engineer who restores 8 HP to surviving crew after a victory. Sable and Latch can be recruited later (see below). They follow the selected character through the ladder graph outside combat. In combat, each is directly controlled and receives their own AP. Vera can be recruited before the deeper Cableworks fights; approach her western balcony before crossing the lower route.

## Build your fighters

PACK is now each fighter’s character sheet. Select the person you want to change, then set their callsign, phosphor color, gun, armor, field gear and weapon modification. Equipment is a collection of **patterns**, not individual rolled items: every fighter can use an unlocked pattern. All changes are available outside combat.

- **Armor:** leathers favor movement; mesh is balanced; breach plate protects at a movement cost; a ceramic firecoat blocks burn; ghostweave adds evasion and speed; Blacksite carapace provides the most protection at the largest mobility cost. Supplier reinforcements add to that fighter’s base armor.
- **Gear:** targeter adds aim; respirator blocks toxin and seep exposure; climbing rig improves route movement allowance; medic rig improves medkits; grounding braid blocks shock; salvage rig adds resources on victory. Each fighter equips one.
- **Mods:** optic rail trades damage for accuracy; hot-load breech trades accuracy for damage; penetrator feed trades damage for armor penetration. One mod per fighter.
- **Talents:** every level grants each fighter one point. Steady Hands improves aim, Hard Hitter improves damage, Tunnel Legs improves movement per AP, and Street Medicine improves medkits. Each has four ranks. Retrain the selected fighter for eight salvage to refund their spent points.
- **Bonds:** three victories together raise a fighter’s bond by one (maximum three); personal-job choices can change companion bonds. Each bond rank adds two accuracy.
- **Grudges:** a fighter downed by an identified hostile remembers them. Until that enemy dies, the fighter deals five extra damage against them. This survives a retreat or defeat.
- **Standing:** helping the wards increases standing. At +3, suppliers discount prices by 20%, rounded up. At -2, prices rise by 15%. Selling Vera’s ledger makes money immediately and costs standing and trust.

The bench is also accessible from PACK as a field-fabrication menu. Craft permanent gear/mod/weapon patterns with salvage. Chests, jobs and cleared districts supply salvage. Talent points and equipment are assigned separately to each person.

## Expanded arsenal and enemies

SMGs spend two rounds per burst. Hand cannons punch armor and knock targets back. Needle weapons apply toxin; flame projectors ignore low cover and apply burn. Arc guns apply shock; grounding braids counter them. Heavy machine guns always suppress. Launchers and plasma guns add friendly-fire blast damage. The chainblade is armor-ripping melee. The dead-letter marksman rifle comes from Vera’s personal job.

Toxin and burn tick once at the target’s next activation. Shock removes an additional AP, stacking with suppression. Enemy stitchers spend an activation healing nearby wounded allies. Demolition runners rush toward the crew and detonate when close. Hounds and chainblade collectors close for melee; drones and gunners seek firing lanes. Major collectors have their own weapons and armor, not just larger health bars.

### Machinery and movement

E/USE near a control performs a safe available operation. Shift+E opens its full panel. If no safe option is available, E also opens the panel. Operations check the selected fighter’s resources and current AP. Most controls cost one AP in battle; lift rides and frags cost two; firing a heavy weapon uses that weapon’s AP cost. Outside combat the resource costs remain, without AP. The furnace’s original high valve is a one-AP shutoff in battle.

The Cathedral cage starts at the bottom. It works only after generator power is restored. Stand close to the cage, operate it, and nearby crew ride together. You can see the crew and cage moving through the shaft. Do not save midway through movement; the game defers such saves until the crew has stopped.

The steam bypass releases a dangerous cloud on the eastern deck. It damages exposed fighters at their activation and costs health in exploration. Turn the same valve off to remove it. Enemy route planning penalizes that active lane when another route exists.

With the grapple owned, press T and tap/click a cyan anchor within 650 units. The TOOLS menu also has AIM GRAPPLE. The active crew use the shared winch. In combat it costs two AP. Revisit the Cableworks roof, Tenement roofline and Cathedral fan housing. The tool follows authored anchors; it does not attach to arbitrary walls. Diving gear grants access to submerged routes rather than adding free swimming. The cutter is a permanent tool; barriers can often be solved another way.

Frags used on structures also hurt nearby crew. Read the control costs before choosing one. Arc repairs consume a real round from the selected fighter’s arc gun.

### Excavator Zero

Two stabilizers shield the core. Shoot a stabilizer or reach its breaker on another floor. Disabling one exposes the core and changes its weapon and protection; disabling both produces its final phase. The core announces which floor its next surge will hit. Change floors, equip grounding gear, bring Latch, or operate the upper coolant valve. Coolant disables the floor surge, not the core’s ordinary gunfire.

Defeating the machine opens the workers’ return tunnel. Read the foreman’s station and settle **The last shift never ended**. It ends a dangerous work order, not the world’s deeper mystery.

Helmets, coats, plates and gear packs change the body. Companions keep identity accents beneath their loadouts.

Use PACK → CREW ROSTER in a room with a safe bunk to bench a companion and choose another. Rook remains active and the travel group stays at three. Reserve members retain gear, talents and bonds. Sable and Latch can wait there if the travelling crew is full.

Vera can recognize the sealed apartment entrance after her ledger job is settled, giving a route without the cutter. Nix’s workshop outcome now discounts pattern fabrication by 20% and is visible at Rustmarket. The alternative clinic outcome retains its medical rewards.

The atlas has a PIN THIS DISTRICT control; pins persist without revealing undiscovered rooms. The safe apartment **Room for One** has a radio story, a packed bag and a bunk. Its mystery needs no gunfight.

## Alpha 4: quick setup and new work

The starter magazine on the low steps east of Mara is an optional movement lesson. Walking/climbing cancels an old click order. A generous ladder grab, short jump buffer and edge grace make the platforms easier to navigate. Followers use the same safe-drop/hop routes. Brief contextual hints can be disabled in SYSTEM.

### Dispatch and recovery

From a work board in Disintegration Ward or Rustmarket choose DISPATCH. Four jobs use a separate three-deck freight site with fixed enemy tiers. The board shows entry costs and next rewards. Tier 2 is an early optional salvage challenge; tiers 3/5 warrant preparation. No enemies automatically grow with your level.

- Recover freight from the upper deck and carry it to the east exit. Each action costs one AP in combat. First clear unlocks the Union focus rail (+11 accuracy without a damage penalty).
- Defend the relay through three enemy rounds or eliminate the attackers sooner. The device has separate health and can take friendly blast damage. E repairs 35 HP once per round for one AP plus three salvage; an active engineer supplies parts freely. First clear unlocks blast liner gear (half incoming explosion damage).
- Escape the elite cordon through the eastern hatch; killing all guards is optional. First clear unlocks the laser.
- Defeat the named collector; surviving escorts do not prevent completion. First clear unlocks the hand cannon.

Saving/import/export is disabled throughout a job. Closing the game returns to its safe pre-entry checkpoint. Return from a completed or withdrawn job to save its actual outcome.

Low on supplies? WARD RELIEF at either safe settlement tops combined spare and loaded ammunition up to 24 and ensures one medkit, without money or XP. Repeated requests cannot stockpile excess rounds. The existing bunk heals freely.

### Longer expeditions

Open the expedition board from J or Ada. Depart from Disintegration Ward, Rustmarket, Kettle Junction or Cable Commons while quiet. Brasslung Mill needs power, Drowned Post needs diving gear, and Carriage Zero needs the freight charter. Each contains three connected areas with recognizable authored geography and saved survey variation in patrols, cache deck and contamination.

The Mill's deep cache grants a survey harness; the Drowned Post grants a recoil cradle; Carriage Zero grants an arc blade.

Return hatches appear in every area. In combat, extracting, collecting, opening a drive or changing expedition areas uses one AP. You need not kill every guard to leave. Short dispatched jobs retain their separate rules.

Expedition kills and caches add **unbanked** credits, salvage and XP. Successful freight extraction banks the haul plus the base reward. Returning without freight banks half the materials and no XP/patterns; defeat banks a quarter and revives downed crew at half HP. Used supplies/entry remain spent. Rewards diminish over five finished trips per family, including withdrawals/defeats; XP only on the first three. After five trips there are no material/XP/cache-supply rewards. First-time permanent patterns can still be found. Full details are in [EXPEDITIONS.md](EXPEDITIONS.md).

Multi-area expeditions can save/export/import outside combat and controlled transit. Their survey, wounds, opened caches, haul and objectives persist. Reloading resumes the same configuration. An active short dispatch still cannot save mid-run. Story districts remain cleared when you return.

### New equipment and presentation

The Union three-stamp rifle trades two rounds per shot for burst suppression. The Switchman's arc blade trades some chainblade armor penetration for ammo-free shock. The recoil cradle adds four accuracy/two penetration; the Redoubt breach feed adds three damage/two penetration. The survey harness adds twenty-five movement units per AP and chemical protection. These use the same visible pattern system, companions and calibration as your existing kit.

New mechanical bosses have physical couplers, distinct machine bodies and their actual phase weapons. Their readouts announce shields and the next threatened floor. The portrait camera reserves space above the tactical bar so the active fighter stays visible near a deep room's bottom.
