# Exceptional equipment catalog / Alpha 7

Patterns are shared by the active crew and reserve. Weapons share ordinary spare ammunition; ordinary switching conserves rounds. None of these discoveries is added to a shop or a normal crafting recipe. The gallery inspects every weapon without granting it.

**Spoilers:** the table names acquisition regions. In-game rumors are less exact. Most cases require all local hostiles cleared; Dead Air additionally needs the recovered cipher. Expedition cases must be successfully extracted with the primary freight. Quiet expedition saves retain unbanked cases; withdrawal and defeat discard them and permit a later retry.

Rumor sources include Nell, the injured courier, missing freight, hoist repair, a residents' dispute, the surgical-machine mystery, the Ossuary receiver and the restored listening station. Entering a region associated with a heard lead marks it investigated. Opening and banking an ordinary case marks it recovered. The Bell and Mercy frames remain investigated until restored at Edda’s workshop. Discoveries keep their flavor notes in the ledger and appear on the personal trophy list.

Exceptional firearm art adds geometry to the corresponding canonical class: a flared bell mouth, suppressor/survey optic, exposed induction coils, unstable feed cage, two-chamber cylinder or industrial fuel pack. Armor and tools also alter the equipped model. Ghost Circuit and Suture Feed have visible feed/coil accents.

## Workshop restorations

For new discoveries, the Funeral Bell and Mercy Killing are incomplete frames. Bank expedition frames with a successful primary extraction. The journal records the frame and missing part; the inventory lists incomplete equipment separately from usable guns. At Edda's completed workshop, the Bell needs the Pump Test Annex pressure governor and six salvage; Mercy needs the capacitor recovered by repairing the Signal Ossuary receiver and eight salvage. Housing Rill reduces either cost by two. Restorations are permanent one-time pattern unlocks and do not create a second randomized item.

Alpha 6 saves that already own these weapons retain them. No already-working item is revoked. The other ten exceptional items keep their acquisition and normal equipment rules.

## Alpha 7 identity audit

All twelve patterns retain their real mechanical effects and visible equipped models. Cards now compare effective damage, accuracy, AP, penetration and range, with magazine and tradeoff information. Disorder comparisons assume a full magazine when inspecting an unequipped weapon; the equipped gun shows its live instability penalty.

The six firearms have distinct recoil amplitudes, muzzle geometry, shot rendering and synthesized pitch/noise combinations: a broad Bell fan, thin suppressed Dead Air tracer, electrical Mercy coils/chain, three Disorder traces, a heavy Widowmaker stroke/impact and the Furnace Key flame cone. Combat messages identify opening-round bonus, burst behavior, chain radius or resource limits. Chaining is limited to 180 units and a clear conductive ray; the struck target's own weapon cannot restrict it. A Forest reduces ranged accuracy and does not protect against melee.
