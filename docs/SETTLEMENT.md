# Disintegration Ward development / Alpha 7

Open **J / JOURNAL → WARD DEVELOPMENT**, the desktop Ward link, or a marked project in the scene. The menu lists costs, deck and unmet conditions. Walk to the project to commission it. Press F to cycle overlapping interactions, then E; touch users can tap a nearby object. Construction takes eight seconds of active time in the peaceful Ward, pauses while away or in menus, and resumes after reload.

The workshop can be developed without advancing the main story. The first successful Service Drains primary delivery gives one machinery unit. Repairing the Sump service hoist or recovering missing Switchyard freight provides another route to machinery. The ordinary benches, rest and shops remain available.

Facility frames, workers and activity appear during/after construction. Completed armory racks, medical beds, workbench, housing, radio aerial, electrical machinery and freight traffic use distinct scene details. Residents and workers never occupy combat or collision slots. The original platforms, ladders, Mara and the cot remain in place.

Projects cannot be commissioned twice. Services require standing at their completed facility outside combat. The workshop's repacking service spends salvage only if at least one crew magazine needs rounds. Facilities and resource expenditure are included in ordinary saves and exports.

## Named services and consequences

Edda appears at the completed workshop and assesses/restores incomplete equipment. The **RESTORATION / RILL** journal page lists the frame, component, cost and current step. A frame can be tracked without accepting the main quest. Existing Alpha 6-owned equipment does not need to be restored again.

Osa arrives beside the freight depot with at most two supply parcels in stock. Each costs ¢24 and contains 18 spare rounds and a medkit. Successful expedition extraction restocks two parcels; reload and withdrawal do not restock them. Stock is saved. The depot's original expedition access and discount remain.

Meda treats up to 40 HP per active/reserve fighter for two salvage at the infirmary and points out a worker in the Gardens. Sib at the listening station connects the receiver mystery to Mercy Killing. Tern at Living Quarters remembers Rill's empty bunk. Rescuing Rill into completed housing creates another named resident, a workshop reunion and lower restoration costs.

Projects show foundation/assembly/commissioning stages during the existing short construction window. Completed roof rails, wall structures, lamps, residents and facility machinery change the scene; the original ladders, floors and service locations stay intact. There is no new waiting grind or second build tier.

The personal bunk now shows actual recovered firearm art, a route-map link, charter/remembrance notes and remarks from recruited companions. The visible rack changes as the collection grows. Equipment remains in the shared crew collection.
