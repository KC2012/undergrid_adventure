# 0.5 — Alpha 4 / The Adventure Loop

- Refined walking, ladder capture, jump buffering, edge forgiveness, camera look-ahead, click-route interruption and companion following. Added safe drop and short-hop edges to shared navigation.
- Standardized nearby interaction ranges, useful-object highlighting and F selection cycling. E chooses a safe available machine operation; Shift+E retains all options. T quickly aims an owned grapple.
- Split PACK into equipment tabs, retained live loadout art and effective comparisons, added two quick weapon slots and tactical swaps conserving ammunition.
- Added Q target cycling, Space fire, direct ability buttons, optional next-ready selection, Crew Watch and faster/default enemy resolution with a steady setting.
- Changed ordinary fights to local patrol groups; major set pieces remain full-site battles. Exactly-once kill rewards persist across local fights, retreats and reloads.
- Added six active abilities and earned licences at levels 2/4/6. Added three paid calibration ranks per fighter/weapon, arsenal acquisition hints and weapon mobility tradeoffs.
- Added four deliberately dispatched objective jobs: recover/extract, defend a repairable relay, escape a cordon and defeat a named collector. Fixed tiers, entry costs, diminishing payouts, first-clear rewards and a separate work site keep cleared story routes stable.
- Added a blast liner and Union focus rail with visible equipment details, ward emergency supplies, a small early vertical stash, richer district props, a working conveyor, cargo-release blast and permanent runoff isolation.
- Added milestone/loot/level feedback and a saved improvement history. Combat notifications and contextual controls avoid covering tactical buttons on portrait screens.
- Fixed orphaned winch/lift movement on selection, restart/import runtime recovery, ordinary movement save footing, stale travel effects, late-recruit weapon unlocks and empty-gun fallback behavior.
- Preserved the exact previous Alpha 3 release. Added fresh-opening, normal-AP dispatch, objective/skill/input/migration tests and updated the existing regressions and player guide.
