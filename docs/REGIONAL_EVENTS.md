# Regional encounters / Alpha 7

Regional opportunities are selected when entering a region. The active encounter and visit count are saved, so reload does not reroll an outcome. Every third visit is quiet; an unresolved encounter can appear again on another eligible visit. Completed unique encounters do not recur. Remote story set pieces receive no ambient events.

| Encounter | Region | Required world state | Choices and consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-pipe scavenger camp | Cableworks | Banked Bell frame or successful Service Drains trip | Listen for the Annex/isolator clue; optionally share six rounds for +1 standing |
| Injured filter worker | Blackwater Gardens | Infirmary complete and local hostiles cleared | Spend one medkit for evacuation, ¢20, three salvage and standing; or give directions for standing without material pay |
| Last freight of the shift | Switchyard Nine | Freight depot complete and local hostiles cleared | Send the crate to medical services for two medkits, or the workshop for six salvage |
| Returning maintenance shift | Drowned Sump | Resolve the existing service-hoist repair | Exchange news; atmospheric, no currency or XP |

Scavenger groups and camp equipment are decorative friendly actors. They do not enter combat, block platforms or change the saved enemy roster. Existing Alpha 6 aid objects and their rewards remain separate. The new encounters are sparse unique opportunities, not a source of repeating unique rewards or a simulated roaming economy.

## The missing engineer

Rill is a separate persistent optional adventure in the Pump Test Annex, reached from the eastern Cableworks residential catwalk. Isolate its induction floor from the upper deck and defeat the test watchers before moving the engineer.

With Living Quarters complete, offer a Ward bunk: Rill appears beside Edda's workshop, restoration costs fall by two salvage, and the reunion at the workshop pays ¢25 and six salvage once. Alternatively, send Rill with the freight crew for one machinery unit. This choice cannot later be repeated or exchanged for the other outcome. It does not change Common Line story flags.
